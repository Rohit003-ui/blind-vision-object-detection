About OpenCV Blind Vision

OpenCV Projects is dedicated to showcasing the power and potential of computer vision technology. Our mission is to provide intelligent assistive software for visually impaired individuals, giving them greater situational awareness through real-time camera processing and text-to-speech audio guidance.

👁️ Real-time Detection

Processes live video feeds using deep learning Object Detection models (SSD MobileNet V3) to identify objects, obstacles, and people instantaneously.

🔊 Voice Commentary

Converts detected object labels into natural spoken audio via text-to-speech engine, allowing users to listen to their surroundings seamlessly.

⚡ High Efficiency

Optimized with Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) and threaded frame generation for lightweight performance across desktop and web environments.
